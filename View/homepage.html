How Lifeline Works

A simple 3-step coordinated process to save lives.

Submit Request

The patient submits a blood reservation request with the required blood group and hospital details.

Admin Approval

The admin reviews stock availability and approves genuine emergency requests.

Receive Blood

Once approved by the admin, the blood is securely provided to the patient.
